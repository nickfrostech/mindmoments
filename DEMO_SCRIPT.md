# 🎬 MindMoments — 90-Second MLH Hackathon Demo Script

> **A step-by-step video presentation guide to captivate judges in under 2 minutes.**

---

## ⏱️ Video Breakdown (Total: 90 Seconds)

### 0:00 – 0:20 | The Hook & The Problem
* **On Screen**: MindMoments opened on mobile view in browser (`localhost:3000`). Show the clean nocturnal canvas and glowing logo.
* **What you say**:
  > *"Most AI journaling apps are just shallow prompt wrappers: you tell an AI how you feel, and it gives you generic, ungrounded advice. They have no real memory of your life, they can't detect when your late-night coding causes afternoon fatigue, and pasting your deepest secrets into closed corporate clouds violates your privacy.*
  >
  > *This is **MindMoments** — an intimate, memory-grounded personal diary and cognitive reflection system powered by Google's open-weight **Gemma 2**."*

---

### 0:20 – 0:40 | The 1-Click Seeder & Universal Diary
* **On Screen**: Click the **"✨ Seed Demo"** button in the header. Watch the timeline instantly populate with 14 realistic diary entries spanning the past two weeks.
* **Action**:
  1. Scroll through a couple of entries: show the mood badges (`🔥 Breakthrough`, `😴 Fatigued`), time badges (`9:42 AM`), and tag chips (`#decision`, `#project`, `#code`).
  2. Quick capture demo: Type *"Finished the hackathon prototype in 3 hours flat"* $\rightarrow$ click `😊 Focused` $\rightarrow$ tap **Log Moment**.
* **What you say**:
  > *"MindMoments stores all memories locally on-device. With our built-in 1-click seeder, we have 14 real moments logged—from late-night debugging sprints to crucial architecture decisions. Let's log a live moment right now. It syncs instantly with zero lag."*

---

### 0:40 – 1:05 | 🔮 Look Ahead: Today's Occurrence Forecast
* **On Screen**: Scroll up to the **Today's Occurrence Forecast Card** at the top. Point out the **Cognitive Rhythm Arc** and pattern warnings.
* **What you say**:
  > *"First, Gemma looks **forward**. Instead of generic astrology or mood graphs, Gemma 2 analyzes your actual historical logs. It detects that after late-night sessions on October 21 and 22, fatigue struck the following afternoon. It predicts a 94% focus peak in the morning and issues an alert to protect the afternoon against context-switching fatigue."*

---

### 1:05 – 1:30 | 🧠 Look In the Moment: AI Mind with Grounded Citations (The Climax)
* **On Screen**: Scroll down to the **🧠 AI Mind** card (or click the bottom tab). Tap the quick chip: *"What patterns trigger my creative breakthroughs?"* or type it and tap **Ask**.
* **Action**:
  1. Gemma synthesizes the answer in ~1 second.
  2. Click on one of the **Evidence Citation Chips** (`📎 Cited: Oct 18 #decision` or `Oct 31`).
  3. Show the citation verification popover displaying the exact source quote!
* **What you say**:
  > *"Now, let's look at the present: **AI Mind**. When I ask: 'What patterns trigger my creative breakthroughs?', Gemma doesn't hallucinate. It reads my recorded memories and proves it with **Verifiable Grounding**. Tap any cited moment, and you see the exact diary entry that informed the reflection. Every claim is anchored in documented facts."*

---

### 1:30 – 1:45 | 📊 Look Back: Weekly Recap & Open-Weight Pitch
* **On Screen**: Switch to the **Recap** tab at the bottom to show the structured Weekly Retrospective (Key Decisions, Breakthrough Milestones, and Open Threads).
* **What you say**:
  > *"Finally, the **Weekly Retrospective** synthesizes your entire week's trajectory into decisions made, wins celebrated, and unfinished threads to carry into next Monday.*
  >
  > *Because MindMoments is built on Google's open-weight **Gemma 2**, your most private thoughts never have to be mined on closed cloud servers. You get human-centric, verifiable self-reflection with complete peace of mind. Thank you!"*

---

## 💡 Quick Tips for Recording
1. **Screen View**: Use Chrome DevTools Device Mode set to **iPhone 14 Pro / 15 Pro** (or centered 420px window) for that sleek mobile-app-on-the-web look.
2. **Audio**: Speak clearly and enthusiastically—judges review dozens of projects, so highlighting **"Verifiable Grounding"** and **"Open Weights"** will make you stand out immediately!
